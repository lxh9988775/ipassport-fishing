Path of the Mage King - User Guide

[Getting Started]
1. Flash the firmware onto your FoloToy AI Passport, reboot, and pick "Path of the Mage King" from the app list.
2. On the title screen, press OK to begin a new adventure and name your young mage.
3. Progress is saved automatically to the device. Quit any time and continue later - no manual saving needed.

[Controls (3-button hardware)]
· Short press OK: confirm / interact / advance dialogue and battles
· Short press Down: move cursor down / next item
· Short press Up: move cursor up / previous item
· Long press OK: go back one level (works on battle result screens and all lists)
· You can never get stuck - long-press OK always exits the current screen

[What's Inside]
· Mage Town: Home (rest & recover), Magic Academy (upgrade spells), Adventurers' Guild (take quests), Forge (craft equipment), Magic Black Market (buy & sell), Residence (alchemy lab and magic research).
· Wild Trials: explore 5 zones, defeat monsters, earn gold, EXP, moon grass, beast bones and magic crystals.
· Capture Partners: use capture orbs or seal cards to befriend cat spirits, slimes and more. Partners fight alongside you, level up, and evolve.
· Turn-based Battles: hit elemental weaknesses for 1.5x damage and break enemy guards; resistances reduce damage. Use potions, capture orbs and seal cards mid-battle, or toggle auto-battle / auto-capture / auto-spell.
· Dungeons & Ruins: unlocked by meeting conditions, dropping rare equipment and crystals.
· Expeditions & Events: special adventures with generous rewards.
· Boss Battles: beat each chapter's boss to advance the story, win equipment, crystals, orbs and seal cards, and unlock the next chapter.
· Monster Encyclopedia: records every monster you have met or captured.

[Kid-friendly Design]
· No death penalty: losing a battle just sends you back to town to rest - you keep all gold and partners.
· Stats never decrease, perfect for children to play independently.
· Full Chinese interface on a 2.4-inch portrait display, no internet required.

## 简介
- 中文（description-zh）：
一款移植到 FoloToy AI Passport 上的中文魔法 RPG。你将扮演一名小魔法师，在魔法小镇升级魔法、打造装备，前往 5 大区域讨伐魔物，用捕捉球和封印卡收服猫灵、史莱姆等伙伴并肩作战。弱点属性 1.5 倍伤害、破防连击、回合制策略战斗，还有副本、遗迹、远征、事件与章节 BOSS 等你挑战。没有死亡惩罚、数值只涨不扣，孩子也能独立畅玩。进度自动保存，3 键即可完成全部操作。

- 英文（description-en）：
A Chinese-language magic RPG ported to FoloToy AI Passport. Play as a young mage: upgrade spells in Mage Town, craft gear, battle monsters across 5 zones, and capture cat spirits, slimes and more as partners with capture orbs and seal cards. Weakness hits deal 1.5x damage and break guards in strategic turn-based combat, plus dungeons, ruins, expeditions, events and chapter bosses. No death penalty and stats never drop - kids can play it on their own. Auto-save and full 3-button controls.

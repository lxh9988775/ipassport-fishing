Offline play — no network setup needed, just power on. UP / DOWN to move, OK to confirm or act, long-press OK to go back.

1. Long-press OK for the menu, move to "Spot" and press OK. Pick a water with UP / DOWN, then press OK to open the spot picker; switch spots left and right, read the "likely catch" hint, and confirm with OK.
2. Back at the water, press OK to cast. Wait for the bite, then press OK as soon as "Bite! Press OK" appears.
3. While reeling, hold OK to raise the catch zone and release to let it fall. Keep the fish inside until the bar fills; never letting it out earns a perfect catch.
4. The long-press menu swaps rods, bait or water, opens the guide, or mutes sound. In the guide, UP / DOWN pages through fish and OK opens details, which show the day and spot of your first catch.
5. Waters unlock as your catch count grows, and locked ones show how many are left. Score and guide progress save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.

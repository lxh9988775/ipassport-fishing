Turn AI Passport into a pocket fishing trip: five waters, 27 species, ten distinct spots — every catch is written straight into your field guide.

Cast from the reed beds and deep bend of the quiet pond, the shallows and backwater of the rushing river, the reef and open water offshore, the mudflats and tidal channel of the estuary, or under the lights of the night pier. Where you stand changes which fish show up most often, but nothing is ever locked away: black bream can still rise from the mudflats, just less often.

Cast, wait for the bite, then hold OK to lift the catch zone and release to let it drop. Keep the fish inside the zone to fill the reel — stay on it the whole way for a perfect catch. Three rods feel different: the hand rod is steady, the lure rod is sharp and rewarding, the sea rod is built for giants. Morning, noon, dusk and night rotate with clear, cloudy and rainy skies, so the same water never looks twice alike.

The guide now remembers where each species was first landed, alongside length, weight, rarity, a short personality note and your catch history. Undiscovered fish stay as silhouettes until you meet them. New waters open naturally as your catch count grows, and your best score and guide progress are saved.

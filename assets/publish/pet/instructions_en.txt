Plays offline — no network setup, just power on. One device holds two plays, Fishing and Bunny; pick one at startup.
Controls: UP / DOWN to choose, OK to confirm or act.
1. Move the highlight to "Bunny" with UP / DOWN and press OK.
2. A bunny gently breathing appears; four green bars show Food, Clean, Fun and Energy.
3. Pick Feed / Wash / Play / Sleep to care for it; pick "Mini-games" for five games like Carrot Catch (long-press OK to exit anytime); pick "Passport" for the sticker collection.
4. Good care earns a level-up celebration; bars fall slowly, so a few days away is fine.
5. Progress saves automatically; restart to switch back to Fishing.

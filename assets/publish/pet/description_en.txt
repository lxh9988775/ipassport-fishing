Pocket Bunny is a virtual pet living in your AI Passport. It never dies and never punishes — leave it for days and it is still fine. Just care for it whenever you like.

Pick "Bunny" at startup to meet a fluffy bunny breathing on screen, with four bars below for Food, Clean, Fun and Energy. Use UP / DOWN to choose Feed / Wash / Play / Sleep and press OK: fill its belly, clean it up, play together, or tuck it in (the screen dims to protect young eyes as it sleeps). Care earns level-ups with a little celebration. Progress saves automatically. The same device also hides a fishing play — restart to switch.

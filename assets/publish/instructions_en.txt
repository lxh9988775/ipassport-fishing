Getting started — no network setup needed, just power on and play:

1. In the menu, use UP / DOWN to switch bait (worm / dough / spinner) and spot (pond / river / deep sea); long-press OK to return to the game.
2. Press OK to cast. Once the float lands, wait patiently.
3. When the float dips and the "bite" prompt appears, press OK right away to strike — time it well to land the fish; strike too early or too late and it escapes.
4. Each catch scores by your current bait and spot. Your best score is saved automatically and carries over to your next session.

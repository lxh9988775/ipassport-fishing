Turn AI Passport into a pocket fishing machine: three waters, 24 species, and a codex that fills itself in as you catch them.

Ponds, rivers and the open sea each hold their own fish, from minnows and crucian carp to tuna and sailfish — every one with its own length, weight, rarity and a line of description. Fish you haven't caught stay as silhouettes until you land them yourself. Cast, wait for the bite, then hold OK to lift the green catch bar and release to let it fall, keeping it over the fish until the progress bar fills. Never let it slip and you earn a Perfect catch. The three rods feel genuinely different: the hand rod is forgiving, the lure rod is harder but scores higher, and the sea rod is your best shot at a legend. Catch enough and the next water unlocks; your best score and codex progress save automatically.

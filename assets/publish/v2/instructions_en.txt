Plays offline — no network setup needed, just power on.

Controls: UP / DOWN to move, OK to confirm or act, long-press OK to go back.

1. The play opens straight on the fishing spot. Press OK to cast, then wait for a bite.
2. When the screen says "Bite! Press OK", press OK immediately.
3. Reeling screen: hold OK to raise the catch bar, release to let it fall. Keep the green bar over the fish until the progress bar fills to land it. Never let the fish slip out for a Perfect catch.
4. Hold OK for about 0.7 s to open the menu: switch rod, bait or spot, or open the Codex to browse what you have caught. Back on the water you can see the new rod in hand and the new bait on the hook.
5. Spots unlock as you go: the pond is open from the start, the river needs 8 catches and the deep sea 20; the menu tells you how many are left. Score and codex progress save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.

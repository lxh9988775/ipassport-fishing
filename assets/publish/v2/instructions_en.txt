Plays offline — no network setup needed, just power on.

Controls: UP / DOWN to choose, OK to confirm or act, long-press OK to go back.

1. The play opens on the fishing spot. Press OK to cast, then wait for a bite.
2. When the screen prompts "Bite! Press OK", press OK immediately.
3. Reeling: hold OK to raise the catch bar, release to let it fall. Keep the green bar over the fish until the progress fills to land it; never letting it slip out earns a Perfect catch.
4. Long-press OK for the menu: change rod, bait or spot, browse the Codex, or turn the last row, Sound, off. Back on the water the rod in hand and bait on the hook change right away.
5. Spots unlock as you go: the pond is open from the start, the river needs 8 catches and the deep sea 20; when locked the menu shows how many are left. Score and codex progress save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.

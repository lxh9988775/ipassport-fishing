This version makes fishing smoother: you can mute the sound, and the Codex now shows how big each fish grows and what it likes to eat.

1. New "Sound" toggle: long-press OK to open the menu and the last row is Sound — one press switches it on or off, and your choice is remembered after a power cycle.
2. The Codex gains a "Size" line: it shows each fish's adult length range and its favourite bait, even for fish you haven't caught yet — a handy hint on what to use.
3. Your personal best is now more visible: the old line becomes a gold "Max 24.5 cm · caught 6", so your record stands out at a glance.

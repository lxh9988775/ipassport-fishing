This version reworks how reeling feels and makes the whole game run smoother. Gameplay, fish species, levels and codex content are unchanged; unlocked records and high scores carry over.

1. Fixed the catch bar sinking when you hold OK: holding OK used to move the bar down the track, the opposite of the on-screen hint "hold OK to lift, release to drop", so pressing felt like it did nothing. Now holding lifts the bar and releasing drops it, exactly as the hint says.
2. Fixed the catch bar getting stuck: after holding OK for a second or two on a big fish, releasing often left the bar pinned at the top until you tapped again. Now it rises the instant you press and drops the instant you let go, no matter how long you hold.
3. Fixed holding OK doing nothing right after the strike: the press that triggers the strike used to be swallowed, so keeping the button held did not raise the bar until you released and pressed again. Now you can hold straight through the strike.
4. OK responds faster: input no longer waits for a full "single click" to be recognised, and quick double taps no longer swallow the release.
5. Smoother rendering: the background, text labels and the reel progress bar are only redrawn when they actually change. Previously the whole screen was repainted every frame, which was most visible while reeling and in the codex.
6. Fixed the sailfish weight in the codex: it was shown as a number smaller than its own minimum, making weight appear to drop as the fish grew longer.

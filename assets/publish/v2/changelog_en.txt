This version fixes the long-press menu and retunes how reeling feels and how hard the fight is: easier to land fish, more responsive controls. Fish, rods, spots, levels and the codex are unchanged, and your codex progress and high score carry over.

1. Fixed the long-press menu: long-pressing OK used to do nothing at all — it just cast the line, so the "hold for menu" hint was impossible to follow. Now holding OK for about 0.7 s on the water opens the menu, and holding OK in the menu or the codex goes back one level.
2. Fixed the reeling direction: holding OK now lifts the catch bar and releasing drops it, exactly as the on-screen hint says. Holding OK used to drive the bar downward instead, which felt like the button did nothing.
3. Easier to land fish: fish swim and dash more slowly. A legend used to dash faster than even the quickest rod could follow, so it was simply impossible to catch; now it can be chased down.
4. Bigger catch bar: all three rods get a taller green zone, so a fish you can see is far easier to cover.
5. More room to start: the progress bar starts higher, so a single slip no longer drops you straight back to zero.
6. Wider strike window: the deep sea goes from 0.8 s to 1.0 s, and the other two spots are more forgiving too, so striking early no longer costs you a fish.
7. More responsive controls: while reeling, the button is sampled every 0.01 s instead of 0.02 s, and release is detected in 10 ms instead of 40 ms, so lifting and dropping no longer feel sluggish.

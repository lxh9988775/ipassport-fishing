Pocket Bunny turns your AI Passport into a pixel-style virtual pet you can carry everywhere.

Your little rabbit lives in a cozy room with a framed picture on the wall, a floor lamp in the corner, books stacked on the desk and a rug on the wooden floor. It strolls around, gets sleepy, and waits for your care.

How to play:
- Everything works with three buttons: up/down to choose, OK to confirm.
- Care for your rabbit right from the home screen: feed it, bathe it, play with it, tuck it in — and watch it grow day by day.
- The menu holds even more: 5 pixel mini-games (carrot catch, egg hunt, memory match, jungle adventure, rhythm drums), a sticker passport, and a brand-new dress-up room.
- In the dress-up room there's a try-on mirror: straw hat, crown, glasses, scarf, flower crown — 5 outfits with live preview. Pick one, wear it, and it stays on even after power-off.
- Playing with your rabbit earns stickers; collect all 12 to fill its very own passport.

No game over, no punishment, stats only go up — gentle enough for kids aged 3 and up. Every time you power on, your rabbit is still there, waiting in its little room.

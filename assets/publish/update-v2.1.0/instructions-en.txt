[Getting started]
1. Hold the power button to boot; your rabbit appears in its little room.
2. On the home screen, press up/down to cycle through 6 actions: feed, bathe, play, sleep, menu, sticker passport; press OK to do it.
3. The rabbit gets hungry, dirty and sleepy — just take care of it. No need to watch it constantly; drop by from time to time.

[Menu and mini-games]
1. Select "Menu" on the home screen and press OK. There are 7 items: 5 mini-games, the sticker passport, and dress-up.
2. Up/down to choose, OK to enter.
3. The 5 mini-games:
   - Carrot Catch: move to catch falling carrots.
   - Egg Hunt: look for eggs hidden in different spots.
   - Memory Match: flip cards to find matching pairs.
   - Jungle Adventure: dodge bushes and keep moving forward.
   - Rhythm Drums: tap the drum to the beat of the notes.
4. To quit a mini-game: press and hold OK to return to the menu — you can never get stuck inside a game.

[Dress-up]
1. Choose "Dress-up" in the menu to enter the try-on mirror; the rabbit is shown large.
2. Up/down cycles through 6 options: none, straw hat, crown, glasses, scarf, flower crown — the preview updates live.
3. Press OK to wear the current outfit. It saves instantly and survives power-off.
4. Press and hold OK to go back to the menu.

[Sticker passport]
Playing with your rabbit randomly earns stickers — 12 in total. Collect them all to fill the passport; earned stickers are kept forever.

[Charging]
Charge with a USB cable when the battery runs low. No internet needed — everything is stored on the device, ready to play anytime.
